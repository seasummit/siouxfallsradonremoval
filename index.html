Yes. Sending a request costs nothing and there is no obligation. You only pay if you hire the contractor, and the price comes from them.

What radon level should be fixed?

The U.S. EPA recommends fixing a home at 4 pCi/L or higher, and considering it between 2 and 4 pCi/L (epa.gov/radon).

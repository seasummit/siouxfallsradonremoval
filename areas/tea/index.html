Getting a radon test in Tea

South Dakota's DANR offers free radon test kits to state residents (it set aside 500 kits) through the link on its radon page. Passive kits that go to a lab and electronic monitors that log hourly readings are both acceptable, as long as they meet EPA proficiency standards.

Eastern South Dakota, including the Sioux Falls area, is EPA Radon Zone 1. Once you have a number, the decision is straightforward: EPA recommends fixing the home at 4 pCi/L or higher. If you are already there, or you would rather have a professional test, a local contractor can help.

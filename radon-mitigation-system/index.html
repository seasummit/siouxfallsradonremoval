Radon mitigation systems in Sioux Falls

Most homes are fixed with a system that pulls air from under the basement floor or slab and vents it above the roof with a small fan (often called sub-slab depressurization). A follow-up test confirms the level came down.

Questions to ask any radon contractor

  • Are you certified (for example NRPP or NRSB)?
  • Where will the pipe and fan go, inside or outside the house?
  • Is a follow-up test included, and what level do you guarantee?
  • What warranty covers the fan and the work?
